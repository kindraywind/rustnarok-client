# Which effect files the client plays for effect, skill and item IDs:
# str <effect id> <file under data\texture\effect\ without .str> <texture folder or -> <follows the unit 1/0> [random from to] [once]
#   (once: plays once on a skill's ground unit, where the rest of the effect starts again until the unit goes)
# misc <ZC_NOTIFY_EFFECT type> <effect id>; skill <skill id> <when> <effect id>; item <item id> <effect id>
# part <effect id> <2D|3D> <name=value;...>: a texture (or a sprite) moving from a start to an end over its duration
# ring <effect id> <name=value;...>: a textured cylinder growing, turning and fading
# spr <effect id> <sprite under data\sprite\이팩트\ without .spr> <follows the unit 1/0> <name=value;... or ->: a sprite's animation
# func <effect id> quake <delay ms> <duration ms>: what the client does in code for the effect; quake shakes the camera
# length <effect id> <ticks>: how long an effect's STR plays (16 ms ticks) when shorter than its keys; on a ground unit it starts again 8 ticks before that (Quagmire's 95: 20)
# unit <skill unit type> <effect id> [once]: what a skill's unit on the ground (ZC_SKILL_ENTRY, rathena's UNT_) shows while it's there (once: played once and kept, not started again)
# rsm <skill unit type> <model under data\model\ without .rsm, or an .rsm2 with its extension>: the model standing on such a unit's cell (traps)
# wav <effect id> <file under data\wav\ without .wav> <delay ms> [random from to]: a sound the effect plays
# hit <skill> <hit effect 1> <hit effect 2>: what a damaging skill shows on its target at each hit (-1 none); skills not listed fall back to the skill lines
# caster <skill> <effect>: what a damaging skill shows on its caster as the packet comes (-1 none); skills not listed show 16
# cast <skill> <effect>: the aura a cast with a cast time glows in under its caster (12 by the cast's element, -1 none); skills not listed show 16 (Bash's burst)
# struck <skill> <caster|target> <effect>: what else a damaging skill shows as the packet comes
# used <skill> <caster|target> <effect>: what a skill without damage (ZC_USE_SKILL) shows
# ground <skill> <ground|caster> <effect>: what a ground skill (ZC_NOTIFY_GROUNDSKILL) shows at its cell or on its caster
# trap <skill unit type> <effect>: what a trap shows at its cell when it goes off (its look turning into 0x8c)
# status <EFST id> <effect id>: what a status (ZC_MSG_STATE_CHANGE*) plays again and again on its unit while it's on
